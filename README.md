# TCS Tracking Guide

A quick guide to tracking TCS parcels online in Pakistan.

## Track your TCS parcel

Find the CN (consignment) number on your TCS booking receipt, then use the free online tracker:

**[TCS Tracking Pakistan](https://tcs-tracking.pk)** — https://tcs-tracking.pk

### How to track

1. Copy the CN tracking number from your TCS receipt.
2. Open [tcs-tracking.pk](https://tcs-tracking.pk) in your browser.
3. Enter the CN number and press Track to view the current parcel status and expected delivery date.

The tool is free to use and works for all TCS shipments across Pakistan.
